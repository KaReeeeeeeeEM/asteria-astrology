# Calculation methods and scope

## Implemented

- Tropical zodiac with twelve equal 30-degree signs.
- Geocentric ecliptic longitudes of Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto using `astronomy-engine`.
- Astronomy Engine's equatorial-to-ecliptic-of-date conversion, with light-time/aberration options from GeoVector.
- Ascendant and Midheaven from local sidereal time and a simplified mean-obliquity formula.
- Whole-sign houses for known birth times and absolute latitude below 66 degrees.
- Planetary retrograde flags based on signed longitude change between 12 hours before and after the instant.
- Longitude aspects: conjunction, sextile, square, trine, quincunx, opposition, semisextile, semisquare, quintile, sesquiquadrate, and biquintile. Orbs are explicit in `src/lib/knowledge.ts`.
- Lunar phase angle, illumination, and the next four quarter-phase events.
- Current-to-natal transits at 2-degree orb, displayed for the most recently saved chart.
- Two-chart planetary synastry at 3-degree orb, including identical planet names across different people.
- Equal-weight element distribution across the ten planets.
- Daily sign reflections from the calculated Moon sign and original rotating element prompts. UTC calendar day, refreshed hourly.

## Time handling

Birth date/time is interpreted in the selected IANA zone using `date-fns-tz`, including historical daylight saving as provided by the runtime's time-zone database. Invalid spring-forward wall times are rejected by round-trip validation. Repeated fall-back hours are inherently ambiguous; the interface tells the user to verify the offset. This release does not offer explicit disambiguation of both repeated-hour occurrences.

Unknown time uses local noon and omits Ascendant, Midheaven, and houses. Moon sign and placements near a boundary can still be uncertain. Public results stay in the browser until the user saves them.

The Ascendant formula is `atan2(-cos(LST), sin(obliquity)*tan(latitude)+cos(obliquity)*sin(LST)) + 180°`, normalized to 0–360. The Midheaven formula is `atan2(sin(LST), cos(LST)*cos(obliquity))`, normalized to 0–360. These simplified angle calculations are educational and should not be presented as identical to a full Swiss Ephemeris chart.

## Educational coverage, not calculators

The library introduces tropical/sidereal differences, Vedic/Jyotisha, Chinese/BaZi, traditional and modern methods, dignities, nodes/eclipses, progressions, solar/lunar returns, and profections. These techniques are explicitly labeled as educational and are not generated by the current calculation engine.

Not implemented: sidereal charts, ayanamsha, dashas, divisional charts, BaZi pillars, Placidus/Koch houses, return/progression charts, lunar nodes, Chiron, asteroids, fixed stars, declination parallels, or predictive event guarantees. “All astrology knowledge” is an open-ended collection of traditions; this release delivers a broad foundation and clearly documented calculator scope.
