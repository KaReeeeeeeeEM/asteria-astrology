Reconnect to calculate a chart or open your dashboard. In the meantime, here’s a small guide to the language of astrology.

Planet · Sign · House · Aspect

A planet represents a symbolic function. Its sign describes a style. Its house describes an area of life. Aspects describe relationships between planets.

The Sun is associated with purpose, the Moon with emotional needs, and the Ascendant with your orientation to the world. Read them together, with curiosity.

Astrology is a reflective practice, not a scientifically validated prediction method. Your choices remain your own.
